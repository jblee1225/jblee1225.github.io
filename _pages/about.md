I am a **Senior Research Scientist** (선임연구원) in the Nondestructive Metrology Group, Division of Physical Metrology, at the [Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science (KRISS)](https://www.kriss.re.kr). I also serve as an **Assistant Professor** of Measurement Engineering at the [University of Science and Technology (UST)](https://www.ust.ac.kr/eng/) and as a **Visiting Professor** in the School of Mechanical Engineering at [Korea University](https://me.korea.ac.kr).

My research centers on **uncertainty quantification (UQ)** for the safety assessment of engineered systems — bridging measurements and computational simulations through probabilistic methods, digital twins, and artificial intelligence. Application areas include nondestructive testing, structural health monitoring of civil infrastructure, and nuclear systems.

**Contact:** [jblee@kriss.re.kr](mailto:jblee@kriss.re.kr) · [jblee@ust.ac.kr](mailto:jblee@ust.ac.kr) · [jb.reliability@gmail.com](mailto:jb.reliability@gmail.com)
